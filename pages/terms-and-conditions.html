Project Engagement

A client project is officially accepted once the client formally approves our written proposal or agreement and fulfills the initial advance milestone payment.

Prior to development kickoff, both parties agree upon: functional requirements, project scope boundaries, milestone schedules, delivery dates, revision parameters, and handover conditions. Material scope variations requested subsequently must be agreed in writing.

Pricing and Payments

All project costs are communicated via individualized quotation. Unless otherwise negotiated in a formal agreement, our standard milestone structure is:

20% Project Kickoff Due on agreement confirmation before development starts.
40% Design & Dev Milestone Due upon completion and client review of initial build.
40% Launch & Handover Due prior to final source code handover or public release.

For international cross-border transactions, banking intermediary fees, processing charges, currency conversion rates, and applicable taxes are handled as designated in the invoice. Development work may be temporarily paused if an agreed milestone payment remains overdue after reasonable notification.
