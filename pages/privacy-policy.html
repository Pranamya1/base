01

Introduction

Kalambe Devs respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, contact us, or engage our software development services, how it may be used, and how it is protected.

By accessing our website, submitting an inquiry, or communicating with us, you acknowledge this policy. If you have questions regarding our data practices, reach out via the contact information provided at the bottom of this document.

02

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information across three categories:

A. Information you provide directly

  • —Full name
  • —Email address
  • —Phone number (if supplied)
  • —Company or brand name
  • —Project requirements & budget
  • —Onboarding credentials & assets

B. Technical information

When you visit our website, hosting or security services may process technical telemetry including:

  • —IP address
  • —Browser type & operating system
  • —Device model & screen dimensions
  • —Access timestamps & referring URLs

C. Payment information

If you engage our services, authorized payment providers process financial transactions. Kalambe Devs does not collect or store full payment-card numbers or sensitive cardholder data on its own servers.

03

How We Use Information

Information collected is utilized exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • —Responding to inquiries and project requests
  • —Preparing detailed proposals and technical quotes
  • —Executing contracted engineering milestones
  • —Processing milestone invoices via secured gateways
  • —Preventing fraudulent submissions and abuse
  • —Complying with applicable accounting & legal mandates
Strict Zero-Sale Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade client or prospect personal information to any third parties or brokers under any circumstances.
04

Legal Basis and Consent

We process personal data on established legal bases, including performance of contracts, legitimate business interests (such as responding to inquiries and maintaining studio infrastructure), and statutory legal obligations.

Where applicable data protection laws require affirmative consent, we obtain it prior to processing. You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal and contractual obligations related to completed or ongoing service agreements.

05

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use essential cookies strictly required for core functionality, asset rendering, and security integrity.

If analytical or performance measurement scripts are enabled in the future, non-essential cookies will be managed in compliance with applicable consent guidelines. You can manage or disable cookie preferences directly in your browser settings.

06

Third-Party Services

We utilize verified infrastructure providers to run our studio and host software deliverables:

Hosting & DNS Edge delivery networks and secure cloud hosting providers.
Email Systems Encrypted enterprise communication platforms.
Payment Processors PCI-DSS compliant gateways for invoicing.
Project Integrations Third-party APIs agreed upon for custom client builds.

Each third-party provider operates under its respective privacy terms and regulatory frameworks.

07

Data Storage and Security

We enforce technical and organizational safeguards to protect data from unauthorized access, loss, tampering, or disclosure. Standard measures include encrypted communication (HTTPS/TLS), restricted access controls, and sanitized repository environments.

While no digital transmission is guaranteed to be invulnerable, we regularly assess our workflow and take immediate measures to address any detected security vulnerabilities.

08

Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as strictly necessary to fulfill the specific purposes outlined, including contract delivery, project warranty support, legal compliance, and tax records. Data no longer required is securely permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

09

International Data Transfers

Because Kalambe Devs collaborates with international clients and relies on global cloud infrastructure, information may be stored or processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence. All transfers are conducted in accordance with applicable legal protection mechanisms.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you maintain legal rights regarding your personal information:

Right to Access Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
Right to Rectification Request correction of outdated or inaccurate data records.
Right to Erasure Request permanent deletion where retention is no longer legally mandated.
Right to Restrict or Object Object to processing based on specific legitimate grounds.

To exercise any of these rights, email us directly. We may verify identity before processing requests.

11

Children's Privacy

Our studio delivers commercial digital solutions intended for businesses and competent legal entities. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. Any inadvertent data collected will be promptly purged upon notification.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational workflows, services, or statutory obligations. Any updated iteration will be posted directly to this URL with a revised effective timestamp.
