( 02 ) — The Atelier

One small kitchen,
no shortcuts.

One room in Clerkenwell, one oven, a marble slab we fight over. Everything is built from scratch in the week it’s delivered — the cake you order on Friday doesn’t exist until Wednesday.

London deliveries only

The postcode checker at the bottom of this page isn’t a gimmick. Buttercream kept out of the fridge for hours starts to slide, and Greater London is the farthest we can drive and still promise your cake arrives level.

01

Seasonal by necessity

Berries from June, forced rhubarb in February, chestnuts through October. Fruit out of season turns watery under buttercream, so we simply don’t use it.

02

Small batches only

Twelve to fifteen cakes a week is our ceiling. Your cake gets its own oven shelf and its own last look before the box closes — no order waits behind yours.

03

Every detail by hand

Petals, piped lettering, sprinkles, ribbon — all of it happens at the bench on the morning it’s due. It’s slower than it needs to be, and that’s the entire point.

( 03 ) — How We Work

How an order
comes together.

01

Consultation

We talk through the occasion, how many mouths, dietary needs, and the colours you have in mind. Most of this happens over email; you’re welcome to visit the atelier too.

Reply within one working day
02

Design

We sketch it — tier sizes, pastel colours, the details that matter to you — and confirm the price before anything goes near the oven.

Sketch first, bake second
03

Bake

Baked fresh, usually 24–48 hours before delivery. Sponges are cooled overnight, filled the next morning, and decorated by hand.

Nothing pre-made, ever
04

London Delivery

We deliver within London ourselves, chilled and level. Nothing goes in a courier van if we can help it.

Single-origin, always

( 06 ) — Good to Know

The questions
we get every week.

No. Our cakes travel in a chilled box in someone’s car, and after ninety minutes or so the buttercream starts to misbehave. The postcode check at the bottom of this page will tell you in a second.

House cakes need 24–48 hours. Bespoke designs need one to two weeks so we can sketch, price, and source the right fruit. In a genuine emergency, email us — we keep one slot free each week.

We regularly bake nut-free and gluten-free cakes, and we’re happy to work around most dietary needs. Our kitchen does handle nuts, flour, and dairy, so we can’t promise zero trace — we’ll always be upfront about that.

In the fridge, in the box, until an hour before serving. Buttercream is happiest at room temperature — take it out, let it relax, and it will taste exactly as intended.

We take a 50% deposit to reserve your date, and the balance on the day of delivery. Card or bank transfer, receipt by email, no surprises.
