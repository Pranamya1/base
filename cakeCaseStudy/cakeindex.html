Engineering Deep Dive

Project Challenge & Delivery

The Client Problem & Bottleneck

Maison de Sucre operated on manual Instagram DMs and WhatsApp messages to take bespoke wedding and event cake orders. During seasonal rushes, this caused major friction:

  • • High order drop-off due to back-and-forth manual flavor and tier pricing quotes.
  • • Kitchen prep confusion and miscommunicated dietary requests on busy weekends.
  • • Zero real-time inventory tracking for high-end Valrhona chocolate and imported butter.

How We Solved It (Our Architecture)

Kalambe Devs engineered a synchronous two-sided system with zero third-party software overhead:

  • • Interactive Cake Builder: Instant real-time pricing calculator for tiers, fillings, dietary tags, and delivery slots.
  • • Kitchen Operations Kanban: Automatic state transitions (New → Prep → Baking → Delivery) with daily ingredient pull sheets.
  • • Zero Latency: Lightweight DOM architecture with instant load times and client order lookup codes.
